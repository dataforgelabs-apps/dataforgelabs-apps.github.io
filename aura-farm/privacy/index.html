Privacy Policy

DataForge Labs built Aura Farm as a casual mobile game.

This Privacy Policy explains how Aura Farm accesses, processes, stores and shares information when you use the application.

Aura Farm has been designed to operate without user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking or a remote backend.

1. Information We Collect

Aura Farm does not collect personal information or transmit personal information to DataForge Labs.

The application does not require you to provide:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Address
  • Date of birth
  • Account information
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Audio recordings
  • Financial information
  • Health information

Aura Farm does not require user registration or an account.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Aura Farm stores a limited amount of game information locally on your device.

This includes:

  • Your Best Aura score
  • Application/game preferences required for the app to function

This information is used only to provide the game's functionality.

It is not transmitted to DataForge Labs.

Aura Farm does not use this information for advertising, analytics, tracking or user profiling.

3. Motion Sensor Data

Aura Farm is a motion-based game.

During gameplay, the application accesses your device's accelerometer.

The accelerometer is used to detect movements such as flicks and direction changes and to calculate your Aura score during a game round.

Motion sensor information is processed locally on your device.

Aura Farm does not transmit raw or processed motion sensor information to DataForge Labs or to third parties.

Motion information is not used for tracking, advertising or user profiling.

4. Internet and Remote Servers

Aura Farm does not require an Internet connection for its core functionality.

Version 1.0 does not use a DataForge Labs backend or remote server to store gameplay or personal information.

Aura Farm does not upload your:

  • Aura scores
  • Movement information
  • Gameplay statistics
  • Personal information

to DataForge Labs.

5. Analytics and Tracking

Aura Farm does not currently use analytics or tracking services.

The application does not currently integrate services for:

  • Behavioural analytics
  • Advertising analytics
  • User tracking
  • Advertising attribution
  • Cross-app tracking

DataForge Labs does not use Aura Farm to build advertising or behavioural profiles of its users.

6. Advertising

Aura Farm version 1.0 does not contain advertising.

The application does not currently integrate advertising networks or advertising SDKs.

7. Sharing Results

Aura Farm allows you to voluntarily share game results or challenge friends.

When you choose to share, Aura Farm may generate:

  • Share text
  • An Aura Farm result/share image

Aura Farm then uses the Android sharing functionality available on your device.

You decide:

  • Whether to share the content
  • Which application or service receives it

Aura Farm does not automatically upload or publish this content.

Once you choose to send content to another application or service, the handling of that content is subject to the privacy practices and terms of the selected third-party application or service.

DataForge Labs does not control those third-party services.

8. Third-Party Services

Aura Farm version 1.0 does not intentionally integrate third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services.

The application may interact with Android system functionality required to operate the application, including the Android sharing system.

If you voluntarily share content through another application, that third-party application operates independently from Aura Farm and DataForge Labs.

9. Permissions

Aura Farm is designed to request only the device capabilities required for its functionality.

The application does not request access to:

  • Your location
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Contacts
  • SMS messages
  • Call logs
  • Calendar
  • Photos or media library
  • External file storage

Aura Farm uses device vibration/haptic functionality to provide gameplay feedback.

Motion sensors are used as described in the Motion Sensor Data section of this Privacy Policy.

10. Data Retention

DataForge Labs does not maintain a remote database containing Aura Farm user accounts, personal information or gameplay history.

Game information such as Best Aura is stored locally on your device.

Local application data may remain on your device until it is removed through normal Android application/data management mechanisms, such as clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application, subject to Android device backup and restore behaviour where applicable.

Because DataForge Labs does not receive or maintain this local gameplay data on its servers, DataForge Labs cannot remotely access or delete it.

11. Data Deletion

Aura Farm does not provide user accounts and DataForge Labs does not maintain a remote store of personal user data for Aura Farm version 1.0.

You can remove locally stored Aura Farm data using Android's application data management functionality or by uninstalling the application, subject to Android backup and restore behaviour where applicable.

There is therefore no Aura Farm online account or remote gameplay profile that requires a separate deletion request.

12. Data Security

Aura Farm has been designed to minimize data collection.

For version 1.0:

  • Personal information is not collected by DataForge Labs through Aura Farm
  • Gameplay scores are stored locally
  • Motion processing occurs locally
  • No DataForge Labs backend stores gameplay information
  • No advertising or analytics SDKs are intentionally integrated

Reducing the amount of information collected and transmitted reduces the amount of user information exposed to remote processing.

Users should continue to protect access to their own Android devices using appropriate device security features.

13. Children's Privacy

Aura Farm does not knowingly collect personal information from users through the application.

Aura Farm version 1.0 does not provide:

  • User accounts
  • Public profiles
  • Chat
  • Direct messaging
  • Online multiplayer
  • User-generated content hosting

If the application's intended audience or data practices change in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be reviewed and updated as required.

14. User Accounts

Aura Farm does not provide user accounts.

Users do not need to register, log in or provide an email address to play.

Because no Aura Farm account exists, there is no online Aura Farm account data to delete.

15. International Use

Aura Farm may be made available to users in multiple countries.

Version 1.0 does not transmit personal information or gameplay information to DataForge Labs servers.

If future versions introduce remote services or personal data processing, DataForge Labs will review the applicable privacy requirements and update this Privacy Policy before or alongside those changes.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Aura Farm may evolve over time.

If a future version introduces functionality that changes how user or device information is accessed, collected, used, stored or shared, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Examples of changes that would require review include introducing:

  • Analytics
  • Advertising
  • User accounts
  • Online leaderboards
  • Multiplayer functionality
  • Cloud synchronization
  • Remote crash reporting
  • A backend service
  • Push notifications
  • New permissions
  • Additional third-party SDKs

The "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy will indicate when the policy was most recently revised.
